PipliPop Privacy Policy

This policy explains how PiplipopAIStudio handles information in the PipliPop Android app and on this website.

Effective date: 9 November 2026

Developer team: PiplipopAIStudio

App: PipliPop for Android

Privacy contact: piplipopaistudio@gmail.com

This policy applies specifically to PipliPop, a collection of games and creative activities for children and families, and to this PiplipopAIStudio website.

Information stored by PipliPop

PipliPop stores app settings and game activity locally on your device. This may include sound and vibration preferences, completed-game progress and scores, unlocked sticker identifiers, coloring progress, and saved Sparkle Studio looks. The app uses this information to provide its features. PiplipopAIStudio does not receive this locally saved app information, and PipliPop does not use an account or a PiplipopAIStudio game server.

You can remove local app information by clearing PipliPop's storage in Android settings or uninstalling the app. Android cloud backup is disabled for PipliPop.

Rewarded ads and Google services

Block Rush may offer an optional rewarded video when a game ends. If the player chooses to continue, PipliPop uses Google Mobile Ads SDK to request and show an ad, and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP), where applicable, to collect and manage privacy choices. These services need an internet connection. The core gameplay in other PipliPop activities works offline.

When Google ad services are used, Google may collect or receive information such as the device's IP address, which may be used to estimate general location; ad and app interactions such as ad views and taps; diagnostic and performance information; and device or advertising identifiers. Google uses this information under its own policies for ad delivery, measurement, service operation, and fraud prevention. PipliPop configures ad requests for child-directed treatment, limits ad content to a general audience rating, and disables personalized advertising. The app manifest removes the Android Advertising ID permission; other Google SDK data handling may depend on the SDK version, device configuration, and applicable privacy choices.

Google is a third-party service provider. Review Google's Privacy Policy and Google's Mobile Ads data disclosure for more about Google's collection, use, retention, and choices. Where Google provides a privacy choices form, it can be reopened using the Block Rush privacy options control when available. Android advertising settings can also be managed in device settings.

Information PipliPop does not request

PipliPop does not provide user accounts, ask children to enter their names or contact details, request precise location, or access contacts, photos, the camera, or the microphone. PiplipopAIStudio does not operate analytics, chat, or cloud synchronization in the app. The app uses internet access for Google ad and privacy-choice services. Development builds may connect to a developer's computer for coding; that is separate from the production app.

Children and families

PipliPop is designed for children and families. When Block Rush offers an ad, ad requests use child-directed treatment and a general-audience content rating. PipliPop does not knowingly ask children to submit personal information. Parents and guardians can contact PiplipopAIStudio with privacy questions or requests using the address below.

Retention and security

App settings and game activity remain in local storage until the app's storage is cleared or the app is uninstalled. PiplipopAIStudio does not keep a server-side copy. Information handled by Google is subject to Google's retention and security practices. Network communications for Google services are protected according to those services' current practices.

This website

This website is a static set of pages. PiplipopAIStudio does not add analytics, advertising, or a contact form to the site, and the site does not intentionally set its own tracking cookies. GitHub Pages hosts the website and may process technical request information, such as IP address and browser/request details, to deliver and protect the service. See GitHub's Privacy Statement for GitHub's practices.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when PipliPop's features, software libraries, or data practices change. The effective date at the top indicates the latest revision. Material changes will be reflected on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests about PipliPop, email piplipopaistudio@gmail.com.
